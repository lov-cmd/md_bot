English: At McDonald’s, we believe in the nutritional principles of balance, variety, and moderation. We believe that eating at McDonald’s can fit into a healthy lifestyle. Our goal is to provide information necessary to make informed decisions.

Topic: Data Accuracy & Disclaimer / 資料準確性與免責聲明
English: Nutrition information is based on standard product formulations and serving sizes. Actual sizes and assembly may vary due to individual preparation, different suppliers, country of origin, and seasons. Product formulations may change periodically.
Chinese (HK): 營養資料是根據標準產品配方及標準份量計算。由於每份食物均為個別製作，加上供應商、產地及季節的差異，實際份量及配方可能會有所不同。產品配方亦可能不時作出更改。

Topic: Animal Ingredients & Cross Contact / 動物成分與交叉接觸
English: Whilst some ingredients may not contain animal ingredients, we cannot guarantee that there will be no cross-contact with ingredients that do contain animal ingredients in our kitchens.
Chinese (HK): 雖然部分食材可能不含動物成分，但我們無法保證在廚房製作過程中，不會與含有動物成分的食材發生交叉接觸。
