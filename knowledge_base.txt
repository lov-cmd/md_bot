Topic: Prompt Engineering
Description: Prompt engineering is the art and science of designing and optimizing inputs (prompts) to obtain the most accurate, relevant, and useful outputs from large language models.

Topic: Zero-Shot Prompting
Description: This is the simplest prompting technique, providing only instructions without any examples. It relies entirely on the model's pre-trained knowledge.

Topic: Few-Shot Prompting
Description: Provides multiple (2 or more) examples to the model, demonstrating a pattern for the model to follow. Suitable for complex tasks requiring specific formats.

Topic: Persona (Role-Playing)
Description: Assigns a role to the LLM in the prompt (e.g., "As a... expert"). This helps control the tone, style, and complexity of the response.

Topic: Temperature
Description: A parameter that controls the randomness of the model's output. Low temperature (0.2) produces deterministic answers. High temperature (0.9) produces creative results.
